## 1. Introduction
ForgeWise AI ("the Platform", "we", "our") provides an enterprise multimodal artificial intelligence knowledge retrieval platform for industrial manufacturing facilities. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information processed across the Platform, specifically addressing uploaded technical documentation, operator queries, voice transcriptions, and system telemetry.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Account & Authentication Data
When an authorized user registers or is invited by an Organization Administrator, we collect:
- Work email address
- Full name and professional title
- Password hash (managed securely through Supabase Auth / Enterprise SSO)
- Assigned organizational role and plant permissions

### B. Uploaded Industrial Documents & Technical Assets
To enable the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant, Knowledge Managers upload proprietary documentation, including:
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) service manuals
- Electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, and P&IDs
- Machine maintenance records and technical tables

### C. Conversational Queries & Chat History
When operators and technicians interact with the assistant, we record:
- The natural language query submitted (in English, Hindi, or other configured languages)
- Associated equipment tags or machine filters (e.g. `PMP-402`)
- The synthesized response and citation links generated by the platform
- Optional feedback ratings and operator diagnostic remarks

### D. Voice Input & Speech Processing
When the operator activates the microphone button:
- Speech audio is converted to text using the browser's native Web Speech API or customer-configured speech recognition provider.
- Raw voice audio recordings are not stored on our servers. Only the resulting text transcription is processed to retrieve answers.

### E. Telemetry & Operational Analytics
To maintain high availability and identify undocumented maintenance topics:
- Query latency and provider inference duration
- Document ingestion success/failure logs
- Frequency of ungrounded or unanswered technical topics
- Security audit logs (login timestamps, user IP addresses, administrative role assignments)

---

## 3. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing
We process data strictly to:
1. Provide grounded, cited technical answers to plant personnel to reduce machine downtime.
2. Maintain strict multi-tenant isolation so no organization can access another organization's proprietary documents.
3. Fulfill workplace safety requirements by surfacing verified Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) warnings.
4. Maintain tamper-evident compliance audit logs.

---

## 4. Artificial Intelligence Model Training Commitment
**We do not use your proprietary documents, schematics, chat queries, or feedback to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundation models.** All LLM and embedding inference is performed under enterprise zero-retention terms where prompts and completions are discarded immediately after processing.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers and Data Storage
Data processed by the Platform is stored in:
- **Relational Database & Vectors**: Managed PostgreSQL (Supabase) with pgvector extension.
- **Private File Vault**: Private, encrypted object storage with access governed by 15-minute expiring HMAC signed URLs.
- **AI Inference Providers**: Configurable enterprise endpoints (e.g., Azure OpenAI, Google Cloud Vertex AI, Anthropic, or on-premise local Ollama instances).

---

## 6. Retention and Deletion
- **Document Purging**: When an authorized Knowledge Manager deletes an SOP or manual, the file, its extracted text chunks, and its mathematical vector embeddings are purged from the primary database immediately.
- **Chat History**: Operators can clear individual conversation threads or full history.
- **Backups**: Encrypted rolling system snapshots are retained for disaster recovery for up to 30 days, after which they are automatically overwritten.
- **Account Closure**: Upon account termination, personal identifiers are permanently deleted within 30 days.

---

## 7. Security Incident Response
In the event of a verified data breach impacting personal or organizational data, our security team will notify affected Organization Administrators without undue delay and provide technical remediation details within 72 hours of verification.
